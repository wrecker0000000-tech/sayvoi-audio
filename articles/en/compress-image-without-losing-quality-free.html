How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality (Free)

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Big photos slow down websites, fill up your phone and fail to upload. The good news is that most photos can lose 70% to 90% of their size without any visible change. Here is how.

Step by step

  1. Open the image compressor.
  2. Choose your photos. You can pick many at once.
  3. Leave the quality at 75. For most photos this looks the same as the original.
  4. Set Longest side to 1920 px. This is more than enough for the web, WhatsApp and email.
  5. Look at the sizes next to each photo, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

What saves the most space

  • Resizing. A phone photo can be 4000 px wide, but a screen shows about 1920 px at most. Reducing the size alone often removes most of the file.
  • Quality 70–80. The eye cannot see the difference, but the file becomes much smaller.
  • The right format. Photos are smaller as JPG or WEBP than as PNG.

Good to know

  • PNG is lossless, so it does not shrink much. Use it only for logos and screenshots.
  • If a file is already small, the tool keeps your original and tells you so.
  • Compression removes hidden data like the camera model and GPS location, which is good for privacy.
  • Keep your original photos. Compression is a one-way trip.
