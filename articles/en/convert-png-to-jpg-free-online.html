Convert PNG to JPG Online for Free

Last updated: November 21, 2026

PNG files are often much bigger than JPG files, and some forms and apps accept only JPG. Converting takes a few seconds.

Step by step

  1. Open the image converter.
  2. Choose your PNG images. You can choose many.
  3. Under Convert to, choose JPG.
  4. If your PNG has transparent areas, pick the background color you want. White is the default.
  5. Download each file, or all of them as a ZIP.

PNG or JPG?

  • JPG is best for photos. The files are small and open everywhere.
  • PNG is best for logos, icons and screenshots with sharp text, and for transparency.

Good to know

  • JPG cannot be transparent, so transparent areas take the background color.
  • Each conversion to JPG lowers the quality a little. Keep the PNG as your original.
  • Use quality 90 for a result that looks the same, or lower it for a smaller file.
