How to Compress a PDF and Make It Smaller for Free

Last updated: November 19, 2026

A PDF that is too big cannot be emailed or uploaded to many websites. This tool makes it smaller on your own device, so your file is not uploaded anywhere.

Step by step

  1. Open the tool and press Choose a PDF file, or drop the file on the page.
  2. Choose a method and a quality.
  3. Press Compress. You will see the original size, the new size and the percent saved.
  4. Press Download the smaller PDF.

The two methods

  • Keep the text: only the pictures inside the PDF are made lighter. The text stays sharp and you can still select and search it. This is best for PDFs with photos.
  • Smallest file: every page becomes a picture. It gives the smallest result, mostly for scanned documents, but the text can no longer be selected or searched.

The quality choices

  • High quality: a small saving, with almost no visible change.
  • Medium: a good balance, and the one we recommend first.
  • Smallest size: the biggest saving, with some loss of sharpness.

Good to know

  • A PDF that has only text is already small. The tool will tell you if it cannot make it smaller.
  • If the first try does not help, try the second method or a lower quality.
  • Open the new file and check the pages before you send it.
  • A PDF with a password must be unlocked first. Your file is never uploaded.
