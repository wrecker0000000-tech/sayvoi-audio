Reduce Photo Size for WhatsApp, Email and Upload Forms

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Many forms say “maximum 2 MB” and a phone photo is often 5 MB or more. You do not need an app to fix it. You can shrink the photo in your browser in a few seconds.

Make a photo smaller

  1. Open the image compressor and choose the photo.
  2. If the result is still too big, set Longest side to 1280 px.
  3. Still too big? Lower the Quality to 60. Watch the size next to the photo.
  4. Download the result and upload it to the form.

Which size to choose

  • Job and government forms: 1280 px is usually clear enough for a photo or a scan.
  • Email: 1920 px keeps a photo sharp on any screen.
  • WhatsApp: it compresses photos by itself, so send a smaller file if you want to control the quality.
  • Documents and ID photos: do not go below 1280 px or quality 70, so the text stays readable.

Good to know

  • The photo is not uploaded anywhere while you compress it. That matters for IDs and private photos.
  • If the form accepts only JPG, choose JPG in Output format.
  • Many photos at once? Download them all as a ZIP.
