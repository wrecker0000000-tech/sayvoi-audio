Tips

  • Some words change with the sentence. For example مش is لا before a verb, and ليس before a noun.
  • The demonstrative goes before the noun in fusha: الكتاب ده becomes هذا الكتاب.
  • Verb forms change too: بيكتب becomes يكتب, and هيكتب becomes سيكتب.
