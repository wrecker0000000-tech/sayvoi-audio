How to Start Recording Voice Data: A Beginner's Guide

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Companies collect recordings of real voices to train speech recognition systems and voice assistants. This guide explains the basics for someone starting out.

What is voice data?

Voice data is a collection of recordings of people speaking, usually following a script or a set of instructions. The recordings are used to teach software to understand speech.

Common project types

  • Reading sentences from a script. See recording sentences from a script file.
  • Wake words and voice commands
  • Dialect and accent recordings
  • Free conversations

What you need

  • A quiet room
  • A phone or a microphone
  • A clear, steady voice
  • Your native language or dialect

For a clean result, read how to record clean audio with your phone.

Follow the specs

Each project has its own rules: file format (often WAV), sample rate, naming, and length. Always follow them exactly. A typical list is in preparing voice recordings for data projects.

Where to look for work

  • Data collection companies
  • Freelance platforms
  • Trusted agencies
  • Communities of recorders

Be careful: never pay to get work

Real projects do not ask you to pay a fee to join. If someone asks for money before giving you work, treat it as a warning sign.

Before you submit

  • Check the audio quality.
  • Name the files correctly.
  • Deliver on time.
