Turn Phone Photos of Documents into a PDF

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You do not need a scanner app. A phone camera and a free page are enough to make a neat PDF of your papers.

Take good photos

  • Put the paper on a flat, dark surface, so the edges are clear.
  • Use daylight, or a lamp from the side. Avoid your own shadow on the page.
  • Hold the phone straight above the paper, not at an angle.
  • Fill the frame with the paper, but keep the four corners visible.

Make the PDF

  1. Open Images to PDF on your phone.
  2. Choose the photos. Put them in page order with the arrows.
  3. Select A4 and keep the margin small.
  4. Choose Medium quality, and press Create PDF.

Good to know

  • For ID cards and private papers, this is a good choice: nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • If the text is hard to read, take the photos again in better light, then use High quality.
  • Need to send it by email? Medium quality usually keeps the file small enough.
