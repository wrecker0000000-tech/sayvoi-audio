Convert SRT to VTT, or SRT to Plain Text (Free)

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Sometimes a website asks for VTT and you only have SRT. Other times you have subtitles and you only want the words. Both take a few seconds.

SRT to VTT (or VTT to SRT)

  1. Open the subtitle converter.
  2. Paste the subtitles or load the file. The tool detects the type.
  3. Under Convert to, choose VTT subtitles (or SRT).
  4. Press Convert and download the file.

The only differences are the comma and the dot in the times, and the word WEBVTT at the top of a VTT file. The tool changes them for you.

Subtitles to plain text

  1. Paste the SRT or VTT.
  2. Choose Plain text (no times).
  3. Copy the text. The numbers and times are removed. This is useful for a transcript.

Fix subtitles that are early or late

  • Type a number in Shift all times. A positive number delays the subtitles. A negative number makes them earlier, for example -2.
  • Convert again. All times move together.

Good to know

  • Tags such as italic marks can be removed with one option.
  • Always keep a copy of the original file.
