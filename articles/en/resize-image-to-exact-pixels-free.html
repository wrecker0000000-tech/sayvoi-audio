How to Resize an Image to an Exact Size in Pixels

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Forms, websites and apps often ask for an exact size, such as 800 × 600 pixels. Here is how to get it without a stretched picture.

Step by step

  1. Open the image resizer and choose your photos.
  2. Under Resize by, choose Pixels.
  3. Type the width. Leave Keep the proportions ticked and the height is calculated by itself.
  4. Check the new size next to each photo, then download.

Width and height together

If you type both numbers with the proportions ticked, the photo fits inside that box without being stretched. For example, a 4000 × 3000 photo in a 1080 × 1080 box becomes 1080 × 810.

If you need exactly 1080 × 1080, untick the proportions. The photo will be stretched to fit, so use this only when you accept that.

Good to know

  • You can also resize by percent. 50% gives half the width and half the height.
  • Making a photo bigger does not add detail, and it may look soft.
  • The new file name includes the size, such as photo-800x600.jpg.
