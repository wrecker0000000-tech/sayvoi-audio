How to Sign a PDF Online for Free Without Uploading It

Last updated: November 17, 2026

You receive a PDF contract or an authorization letter and need to sign it quickly. Printing, signing and scanning takes time. And many online signing sites ask you to upload the document to their servers. With Sayvoi, the PDF is opened and signed inside your browser, so it never leaves your device.

Step by step

  1. Press Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  2. Press Add signature. Draw it with your finger, a pen or the mouse. Or choose Upload an image and pick a photo of your signature.
  3. Choose the ink color and the pen thickness, then press Use this signature.
  4. Drag the signature to the right place. Drag the round handle to make it bigger or smaller.
  5. Use the arrows to move to other pages, and sign each one if needed.
  6. Press Download the signed PDF.

Other buttons

  • Add text: type a name, a title or a note, in Arabic or English.
  • Add today's date: puts the date on the page.
  • Smaller and Larger: change the size of the selected item.
  • Edit text: change the words of a text item.
  • Copy to all pages: puts the same item on every page. This is useful for initials.
  • Delete: removes the selected item.

Saved signatures

If you keep Save this signature on this device checked, the signature is stored in your browser only. Next time, tap it to place it without drawing again. You can delete a saved signature with the red button.

Good to know

  • This puts an image of your signature on the PDF. It is not a certified digital signature.
  • If a body asks for a certified electronic signature, use the service they require.
  • Open the signed file and check it before you send it.
