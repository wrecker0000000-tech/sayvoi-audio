How to Practice English Pronunciation Online for Free

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You do not need a teacher or an app to start improving your English pronunciation. You need three things: hear the sentence correctly, say it out loud, and check yourself. This free page does all three in your browser.

Step by step

  1. Open the pronunciation trainer and type the sentences you want to practice, one per line. You can also upload a .txt file.
  2. Choose the accent: American or British.
  3. Press Listen. Start with the Slow speed, then move to Normal and Fast.
  4. Press Record and say the sentence. Press Stop when you finish.
  5. Look at the result. Green words were clear. Red words were missed, so listen to them again and repeat.
  6. Press My recording to hear yourself, then listen to the original once more and compare.

What the score means

The score shows how many words your phone understood. If the phone understands you, a listener probably will too. It is not a judge of your accent, and it can be wrong when the room is noisy. Use it as a guide, not as a grade.

Tips for better practice

  • Practice in a quiet room, with the phone close to your mouth.
  • Choose short sentences first, from five to ten words.
  • Repeat each sentence three times before moving to the next one.
  • Pick one accent and stay with it for a few weeks, so your ear gets used to it.

Good to know

  • The voice you hear belongs to your phone or computer, so it can sound different on another device.
  • Your recording stays on your device. The speech score uses your browser’s speech recognition, and in Chrome that sends your voice to Google to turn it into text.
  • The score works best in Chrome. Listening and recording work in most browsers.
