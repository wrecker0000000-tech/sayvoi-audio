How to Convert PDF to an Editable Word File for Free, Including Arabic

Last updated: November 19, 2026

You have a PDF and you need to edit its text. This tool reads the text inside the PDF and builds a Word file you can edit. It runs on your device, so your document is not uploaded.

Step by step

  1. Open the tool and choose the PDF to Word tab.
  2. Press Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  3. Choose the text direction, the page breaks and the text size.
  4. Read the text in the preview. It shows how many pages and paragraphs were found.
  5. Press Download Word file, and open it in Word.

Which option to choose

  • Text direction: Automatic follows the main language of each paragraph. Choose Right to left or Left to right to force one direction.
  • Page breaks: keep the original pages, or make one continuous text.
  • Text size: keep the original sizes, or use one normal size.

What it does and does not do

  • It takes out the text, joins lines into paragraphs, and finds headings by their larger size.
  • It does not copy the exact layout. Pictures, tables, columns, headers and footers are not kept.
  • A scanned PDF is only pictures of pages. It has no text, so it cannot be converted. It needs OCR.
  • A PDF protected with a password must be unlocked first.
  • Some PDFs store Arabic in a way that scrambles letters or word order. Check the Arabic text after converting.
