How to Make a Free QR Code With Your Logo and Colors

Last updated: November 19, 2026

A QR code lets people open your website, your menu or your contact details by pointing their phone camera at it. This tool makes one in seconds, on your own device, and the code never expires.

Step by step

  1. Open the tool and choose Link.
  2. Paste your website address.
  3. Choose the code color and the background color. A dark code on a light background is the safest.
  4. Choose the dot shape and the corner shape.
  5. Press Add a logo, and choose a small or medium size.
  6. Choose the download size, then press Download PNG or Download SVG.

PNG or SVG?

  • PNG is a normal picture. Use it for social media, documents and screens. Choose 1024 or 2048 for print.
  • SVG never gets blurry at any size. Use it for big posters and for designers.

Tips for a code that scans well

  • Keep strong contrast between the code and the background.
  • Keep the logo small or medium. A big logo hides too much of the code.
  • Leave an empty border around the code when you print it.
  • Always test it with your phone camera before you print.

Static codes

These codes keep your data inside the picture itself. They have no scan limit and they never stop working. But you cannot change a code after you print it, so check the address first.
