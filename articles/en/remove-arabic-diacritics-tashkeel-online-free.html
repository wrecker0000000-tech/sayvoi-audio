How to Remove Arabic Diacritics (Tashkeel) from Text for Free

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Arabic text often comes with diacritics (tashkeel) such as fatha, damma, kasra and shadda, and with the stretching line called tatweel. They can break searching, comparing and many tools. You can remove them in a few seconds.

Step by step

  1. Open the Arabic text cleaner.
  2. Paste your text. You can also load a .txt file.
  3. Keep Remove diacritics and Remove tatweel turned on. They are on by default.
  4. The result appears right away. Press Copy or Download .txt.

Example

The text إِنَّ الْعِلْمَ نُورٌ becomes إن العلم نور, and مــــرحبـــا becomes مرحبا.

Good to know

  • Removing diacritics can change how a word is read, for example in the Quran or in poetry. Keep a copy of the original.
  • The tool also removes hidden direction marks that sometimes break a line in the wrong place.
  • Your text stays on your device.
