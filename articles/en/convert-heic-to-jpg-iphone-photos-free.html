How to Convert HEIC to JPG for Free (iPhone Photos)

Last updated: November 21, 2026

iPhones save photos as HEIC to use less space. The problem is that many computers, websites and forms cannot open HEIC. Converting to JPG fixes it.

Step by step

  1. Send the photos to your computer, or work directly on your phone.
  2. Open HEIC to JPG.
  3. Choose your HEIC photos. You can choose many.
  4. Keep JPG and quality 90, or choose PNG.
  5. Download each photo, or all of them as a ZIP.

Good to know

  • The first conversion loads the converter, about 1.3 MB. After that it is quick.
  • The JPG is usually bigger than the HEIC. That is normal, because HEIC is more compact.
  • If a file is already JPG, the tool tells you it does not need converting.
  • Your photos stay on your device, which matters for private pictures.

Stop getting HEIC photos

On an iPhone, open Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be saved as JPG.
