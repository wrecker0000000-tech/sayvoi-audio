How to Reduce the Size of a Scanned PDF for Email

Last updated: November 19, 2026

A scan from a phone app or a scanner can be 10 MB or more for only a few pages. Many email services stop at about 20 MB, and some websites accept much less. Here is how to fix it.

Why scanned files are big

A scan is a picture of each page, saved at a high resolution. The pictures take most of the space. A normal text PDF with the same number of pages is far smaller.

What to do

  1. Open the compress tool and choose your scanned PDF.
  2. Choose the method Smallest file. A scan has no real text to keep, so nothing is lost.
  3. Start with Medium quality and press Compress.
  4. Look at the saved percent. If the file is still too big, choose Smallest size and compress again.
  5. Download the file, open it and check that the text is easy to read.

Tips

  • Do not choose a lower quality than you need. Small text and stamps can become hard to read.
  • If a scan has only a few important pages, take out those pages first.
  • For a mix of several scans, merge them and then compress.

The tool runs on your device. Your documents are never uploaded, which matters for IDs, contracts and invoices.
