Improve Your English Pronunciation With Slow Speed and Recording

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Many learners listen to English all day but never hear themselves. The fastest improvement often comes from a small routine: listen slowly, repeat, record, and compare.

The routine

  1. Listen at slow speed. Slow speech lets you hear every sound, especially the small words and the endings of words.
  2. Repeat out loud, without recording. Say the sentence two or three times, copying the rhythm.
  3. Record yourself. Now say it once for the recording, and look at the words that were missed.
  4. Listen to your recording. Then listen to the original again. The difference is usually easy to hear.
  5. Move up the speed. When the slow version is easy, try Normal, then Fast.

Why each speed matters

  • Slow trains your mouth to make each sound clearly.
  • Normal is how people really speak, so practice most of your time here.
  • Fast helps your ear, so you can follow natural speech in films and calls.

Use the red words

After each recording the trainer marks the words your phone did not understand. Write down the red words. They are often the same few sounds again and again. Practice those words alone, then put them back into the sentence.

Good to know

  • Ten minutes every day is better than one hour once a week.
  • Choose sentences you will really use, such as for work, travel or exams.
  • The score is a guide. It shows whether the phone understood your words, not whether your accent is perfect.
