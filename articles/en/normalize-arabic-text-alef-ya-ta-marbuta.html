Normalize Arabic Text: Unify Alef, Ya and Ta Marbuta (Free)

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The same Arabic word can be written in different ways: with أ or إ or ا, with ى or ي, with ة or ه. For a person this is normal. For a search box, a spreadsheet or a program, they are different words. Normalizing makes them the same.

Step by step

  1. Open the Arabic text cleaner and paste your text.
  2. Turn on Unify alef to change أ إ آ into ا.
  3. Turn on Change ى to ي and, if you need it, Change ة to ه.
  4. Choose how to show the numbers: Western (0 1 2) or Arabic (٠ ١ ٢).
  5. Turn on Extra spaces and Duplicate lines to tidy the list.
  6. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

When it helps

  • Searching and matching names and sentences in a list.
  • Comparing a transcript with a reference text.
  • Preparing text for a database or a spreadsheet.
  • Cleaning text copied from a PDF. Turn on the option for joined letter forms.

Good to know

  • Do not use these changes on text that must stay spelled correctly, such as a book or a final script.
  • Always keep the original. Work on a copy.
