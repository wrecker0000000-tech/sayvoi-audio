How to Merge PDF Files Into One for Free, Without Uploading

Last updated: November 19, 2026

You have several PDF files and need to send them as one. Many online tools upload your files to a server. This tool merges them on your own device, and you can check every page before you save.

Step by step

  1. Open the tool and press Choose PDF files. You can select several files at once.
  2. Each page appears as a small picture, with a tag that shows which file it came from.
  3. Press Choose PDF files again to add more files whenever you need.
  4. Arrange the pages. Use the arrows on a page to move it, or drag it on a computer.
  5. Rotate a page that is sideways, or delete a page you do not need.
  6. Press Download all pages as one PDF.

What the buttons do

  • Select all, Select none: tick every page or none.
  • Rotate selected: turn the ticked pages left or right.
  • Delete selected: remove the ticked pages.
  • Reverse order: flip the order of all pages.
  • Undo: go back one step.

Good to know

  • The pages keep their text and pictures. The tool does not change them.
  • Forms, bookmarks and links may not survive in the new file.
  • A PDF with a password must be unlocked first.
  • Your files are never uploaded.
