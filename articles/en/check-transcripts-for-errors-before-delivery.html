How to Check Transcripts for Errors Before You Deliver Them (Free)

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Transcription projects have strict rules, and one stray symbol or extra space can make a line fail the client review. Reading hundreds of lines by eye is slow. The transcript checker reads every line for you and shows the line number of each problem.

Step by step

  1. Open the transcript checker.
  2. Paste the transcripts, one line for each recording. You can also load a .txt or .csv file.
  3. Choose what to check. For example, turn on Punctuation marks if the project does not allow punctuation.
  4. Add the tags the project allows, such as [noise] and [laugh]. Any other tag is reported.
  5. Press Check and read the report.
  6. Press Fix the safe problems, read the fixed text, then copy it or download it.

What it finds

  • Numbers written as digits.
  • Unknown symbols, such as emoji, and hidden characters.
  • Diacritics, extra spaces and empty lines.
  • Latin letters inside Arabic lines.
  • Repeated words and stretched letters.
  • Lines that are too short or too long.

Good to know

  • Numbers, repeated words and tags are only reported, because you decide what is right.
  • Your text stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
