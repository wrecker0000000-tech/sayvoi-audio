How to Record Narration for a Script Video

Last updated: November 16, 2026

A simple video works well when the voice is clear and the slides match it. These tips help you record narration that fits the Sayvoi Script to Video tool, or any slide video.

Write for the ear

  • Use short sentences. One idea per sentence.
  • Put each sentence on its own line, so each one becomes one slide.
  • Read the script aloud once before recording.

Record it well

  1. Pick a quiet, soft room. See how to reduce echo and noise.
  2. Keep the microphone at the same distance, about a hand span from your mouth.
  3. Read the whole script in order, in one file, with a short pause between sentences.
  4. If you make a mistake, pause, and repeat the sentence. You can cut it afterwards.

Clean it before you use it

Remove long silences, lower steady noise and balance the volume with the audio cleaner. If the voice is too fast or too slow, try the speed changer in the pitch-keeping mode.

Match the slides to the voice

In narration mode, the tool gives each slide time in proportion to the length of its sentence. So the closer your pauses are to the sentence length, the better the match. Always preview before you make the final video.
