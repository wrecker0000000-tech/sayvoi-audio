How to Check a Voice Recording Delivery Before You Send It (Free)

Last updated: November 25, 2026

A delivery can be rejected for small reasons: one missing sentence, a file with the wrong name, or a recording saved at the wrong sample rate. Finding these by hand in hundreds of files takes hours. The delivery checker does it in a few seconds.

Step by step

  1. Open the delivery checker.
  2. Drop your ZIP into the page. Folders inside the ZIP are fine.
  3. Write the file name pattern, for example {speaker}_{gender}_{age}_{code}_{take}.
  4. Paste the expected file names, one per line. This is how the page knows what is missing.
  5. Choose the audio settings the client asked for, such as 44100 Hz, mono and 16 bit.
  6. Press Check. You get a report and you can download it as a CSV file.

What the report shows

  • Missing: names in your list that are not in the ZIP.
  • Extra: files in the ZIP that are not in your list.
  • Wrong name: files that do not follow the pattern, or use a value that is not allowed.
  • Duplicates: the same name twice, or the same recording saved under two names.
  • Wrong settings: sample rate, channels, bits or length outside what you asked for.
  • Empty or broken: files with no audio.

Good to know

  • Sample rate, channels, bits and length are read from WAV files. For other formats the page checks the format only.
  • Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
