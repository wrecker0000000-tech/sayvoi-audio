How to Turn a Script into a Simple Video for Free

Last updated: November 16, 2026

You do not need editing software to make a simple explainer video. If you have a script, you can turn each sentence into a slide, add your own voice, and download a finished video. This guide shows the steps.

What the tool does

  • Splits your script into sentences, and each sentence becomes one slide.
  • Adds a colored background or your own images, with a slow zoom.
  • Times the slides by reading speed, or by the length of your narration file.
  • Records the video on your device and lets you download it.

Step by step

  1. Write or paste your script. Put each idea on its own line, with short sentences.
  2. Choose the video size: wide for YouTube, tall for Shorts and Reels, or square.
  3. Choose colors and text size, and add background images if you want.
  4. Choose silent mode, or add your narration file so the slides follow your voice.
  5. Press Preview, then Make the video, and keep the tab open until it finishes.

Good to know

The video is recorded in real time, so a one-minute video takes about one minute. The file is MP4 or WebM depending on your browser, and both can be uploaded to YouTube. The tool does not create AI voices or AI images. It uses what you give it, and everything stays on your device.

Ideas for what to make

  • Short explainers and tutorials.
  • Training and onboarding slides for a team.
  • Quote and tip videos for social media.
