How to Delete Text, Names or Numbers From a PDF for Free

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Sometimes you need to remove a sentence, a price, a name or a phone number from a PDF before you share it. This tool does that on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

Delete a whole line

  1. Open the tool and choose your PDF.
  2. Tap the line you want to remove.
  3. Press Delete this text.

Delete only one word or number

You have two easy ways.

  • Tap the line, remove the word from the text box, and press Apply. The tool writes the line again without it.
  • Or press Erase area and drag a box over the word. The box takes the color of the page.

Check the result

Use the zoom buttons to look closely. Then press Download edited PDF and open the file once to make sure the page looks clean.

An important warning

This tool covers the text, it does not remove it from inside the file. Someone who knows how could still copy the hidden text out of the PDF. If the text is truly secret, such as an ID number, do not rely on this. Print the page, or save it as a picture, and use that file instead.

Good to know

  • Press Undo if you cover the wrong place.
  • On a scanned PDF, use Erase area. There is no text to tap.
  • Your file is never uploaded.
