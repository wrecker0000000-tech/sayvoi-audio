How to Combine Images into One PDF (Free, Online)

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Many offices and websites ask for “one PDF file” when you have ten photos. You can make that file in your browser without installing anything.

Step by step

  1. Open Images to PDF.
  2. Choose your photos. They appear in a list with small previews.
  3. Use the ↑ and ↓ arrows to put them in order. Press ✕ to remove one.
  4. Choose the page size: A4, Letter, or the same size as each image.
  5. Press Create PDF. The file downloads on your device.

Settings that help

  • Orientation: Automatic turns a wide photo into a landscape page and a tall photo into a portrait page.
  • Margin: None fills the page. Small or medium leaves white space around the image.
  • Quality: Medium is right for most uses. High is better for printing. Low gives the smallest file for email.

Good to know

  • Your images are not uploaded. The PDF is created on your device.
  • If the PDF is too big for a form, choose Low quality, or compress the photos first.
  • You can give the file your own name before you press Create PDF.
