Convert JPG to WEBP Online: Smaller Images for Your Website

Last updated: November 21, 2026

WEBP is an image format made for the web. At the same quality, a WEBP file is usually 25% to 35% smaller than a JPG. Smaller images make a website load faster.

How to convert JPG to WEBP

  1. Open the image converter.
  2. Choose your JPG or PNG images.
  3. Under Convert to, choose WEBP.
  4. Keep the quality at 80 to 90. Check the size next to each file.
  5. Download the files, or all of them as a ZIP.

When WEBP is a good choice

  • Website and blog images, where speed matters.
  • Images with transparency, where WEBP is smaller than PNG.
  • Any place that accepts WEBP, such as modern websites and phones.

When to stay with JPG

  • When you send the photo to someone and you are not sure their app can open WEBP.
  • When a form or an old program accepts only JPG or PNG.

Good to know

  • If a result is bigger than your original, just keep the original.
  • Converting repeatedly lowers quality. Always convert from your original file.
