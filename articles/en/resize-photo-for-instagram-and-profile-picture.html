Resize a Photo for Instagram, Profile Pictures and Websites

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Every platform likes a different size. If you upload a photo that is too large, it can be cut or look blurry. Resize it first and you control the result.

Common sizes

  • 1080 × 1080: a square post.
  • 1920 × 1080: a Full HD picture, a cover or a presentation slide.
  • 1280 × 720: an HD picture, such as a video thumbnail.
  • 500 × 500: a profile picture.
  • 300 × 300: a small avatar or icon.

How to do it

  1. Open the image resizer and choose your photos.
  2. Tap one of the ready sizes under the options.
  3. With the proportions ticked, the photo fits inside the size without a stretch.
  4. Download the results, or all of them as a ZIP.

Good to know

  • A photo with a different shape will not fill the whole square. The longest side matches your size, and the other side is smaller. Crop it on the platform if you need to fill the frame.
  • Choose JPG for photos and PNG for graphics with sharp text.
  • Your photos stay on your device.
