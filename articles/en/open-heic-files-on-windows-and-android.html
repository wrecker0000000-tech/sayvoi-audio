How to Open HEIC Files on Windows and Android

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You received a photo that ends in .heic and it does not open. This is common: HEIC is the iPhone photo format, and many systems do not support it.

Why it happens

  • HEIC is a compact image format used by iPhones.
  • Windows often needs an extra add-on to open it, and some Android apps cannot show it.
  • Many websites and forms accept only JPG or PNG, so they reject HEIC.

The quick fix

  1. Open HEIC to JPG in your browser.
  2. Choose the .heic file.
  3. Download the JPG. It opens on any device and can be uploaded anywhere.

Good to know

  • You can convert many photos in one go and download them as a ZIP.
  • The conversion happens on your device, so your private photos are not uploaded.
  • Ask the sender to change the iPhone camera format to Most Compatible, or send you the photos as JPG.
