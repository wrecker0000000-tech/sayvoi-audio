How to Fix Common Arabic Spelling Mistakes in Transcripts

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Some Arabic mistakes appear again and again in typed text, especially when people write fast or type on a phone. Three are very common: leaving out the hamza on the alef, writing the ta marbuta as ha, and writing the alef maqsura as ya.

Examples the tool fixes

  • الى becomes إلى, and انا becomes أنا.
  • ان شاء الله becomes إن شاء الله.
  • مدرسه becomes مدرسة, and الساعه becomes الساعة.
  • حتي becomes حتى, and معني becomes معنى.

Step by step

  1. Open the transcript checker and paste your text, one line for each sentence.
  2. Keep Common Arabic spelling mistakes turned on.
  3. Press Check. The report shows the line and the correction, for example مدرسه → مدرسة.
  4. Press Fix the safe problems and copy the result.

Good to know

  • The tool uses a short list of very common mistakes. It is not a full spell checker, so read the result before you send it.
  • Words that can be correct in two ways, such as ان and إن, are left alone on purpose.
  • Your text stays on your device.
