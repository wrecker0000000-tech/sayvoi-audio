How to Combine Audio Files into One (MP3, WAV, M4A) for Free

Last updated: November 23, 2026

You may have a lecture in three parts, or a song and an intro that must become one file. You do not need a heavy program. You can do it in your browser.

Step by step

  1. Open the audio joiner.
  2. Add your files. You can add many at once, or a ZIP file.
  3. Put them in the right order with the arrows. Press Sort by name to order them automatically.
  4. Choose how much silence you want between files. Leave 0 to join them directly.
  5. Choose WAV for the best quality or MP3 for a smaller file.
  6. Press Join the files, listen to the result, then download it.

Good to know

  • You can mix formats, for example MP3 with WAV and M4A.
  • Files with different sample rates or one channel next to two channels are matched for you.
  • Your original files are never changed.
  • Choose WAV if you will edit the file again. MP3 compresses the sound once more.
