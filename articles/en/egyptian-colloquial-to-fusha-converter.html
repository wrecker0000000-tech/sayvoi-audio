How to Convert Egyptian Arabic to Standard Arabic (Fusha)

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Many texts are written the way people speak: chats, voice transcripts, notes and interviews. When you need the same text in Modern Standard Arabic (fusha), changing every word by hand is slow. A converter does the common replacements for you, and you review the result.

What the converter changes

  • Words: for example كده becomes هكذا, and دلوقتي becomes الآن.
  • Verb forms: بيروح becomes يذهب, and هيجيب becomes سيجلب.
  • Negation: مش هيجي becomes لن يأتي.
  • Word order: الكتاب ده becomes هذا الكتاب.
  • أن: عايز اروح becomes أريد أن أذهب.

Step by step

  1. Paste the colloquial text, or press Load an example.
  2. Press Convert to Fusha.
  3. Read the result. Blue words were changed.
  4. If a blue word has a dotted line, it has other options. Click it to switch.
  5. Press Copy the text.

Add your own words

Words the dictionary does not know stay as they are. In the box called My own words, write one pair per line, like this: colloquial = fusha. Press Save my words. They are stored in your browser only. You can export them to a file and import them on another device.

Good to know

  • This version covers Egyptian Arabic.
  • It replaces words and common verb forms. It does not correct every grammar rule, so always read the result.
  • Your text and your words stay on your device and are not uploaded.
