How to Convert Text to SRT Subtitles (Free, Online)

Last updated: November 22, 2026

An SRT file is the standard subtitle file. YouTube, Facebook, VLC and almost every editing program can read it. If you already have the text, you do not need to type the times by hand.

Step by step

  1. Open the subtitle converter.
  2. Paste your text. Write one line for each subtitle.
  3. Under Convert to, choose SRT subtitles.
  4. Choose how to time the lines: the same seconds for every line, or by the number of words.
  5. Press Convert, then download the .srt file.

How to get good timing

  • Keep each line short, about 6 to 12 words, so it is easy to read.
  • A line of 3 seconds suits most speech. Use By the number of words when your lines have very different lengths.
  • The times are an estimate. Open the file with your video and shift it if it starts too early or too late.

Good to know

  • Arabic and other languages work. If the Arabic looks broken in a player, keep the UTF-8 option on.
  • For exact timing of each word, use a subtitle editor after this step.
  • Your text stays on your device.
