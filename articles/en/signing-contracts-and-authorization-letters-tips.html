Tips for Signing Contracts and Authorization Letters Digitally

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Signing on a screen is fast, but small mistakes can cause problems later. These tips help you sign contracts and authorization letters with care.

Before you sign

  • Read the whole document, including the last page and any attachments.
  • Check the names, dates, amounts and the scope of the authorization.
  • Keep a copy of the original file before you sign.

While you sign

  • Place the signature on the line meant for it, not over the text.
  • Add the date next to the signature. Use the Add today's date button.
  • If the contract asks for initials on every page, add them once and press Copy to all pages.
  • Type your printed name under the signature if the document needs it.

Know what kind of signature is required

An image of your signature on a PDF is accepted for many ordinary agreements. Some bodies, such as courts, banks or government offices, may require a certified electronic signature or a signature in person. Rules differ by country and by organization, so ask what they accept before you rely on it.

After you sign

  • Open the signed file and check every page.
  • Save it with a clear name, for example: contract-company-2026-signed.pdf.
  • Send it by a channel you trust, and keep a copy.
